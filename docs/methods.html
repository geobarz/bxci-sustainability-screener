Methods & limitations

BCRED Sector Emissions-Intensity Screen

← Back to the dashboard

What this is

A screen of the sector-level emissions intensity of Blackstone Private Credit Fund (BCRED, SEC CIK 1803498), built only from public filings and public emission factors. It shows where BCRED’s money sits by industry, and how emissions-intensive those industries are on average.

NoteSector-level proxy, not financed emissions

The indicator multiplies BCRED’s exposure to each sector by that sector’s average supply-chain emissions per dollar of output. It says nothing about the actual emissions of any borrower, and it is not a PCAF-style financed-emissions figure.

Data sources

All files were downloaded on November 5, 2026.

  • SEC BDC Data Sets: BCRED’s schedule of investments (soi.tsv) and tagged financial data (num.tsv), 16 quarterly or monthly files. Landing page
  • SEC EDGAR submissions API: BCRED’s list of 10-K and 10-Q filings. API documentation
  • EPA Supply Chain GHG Emission Factors v1.3 (July 2024): kg CO2e per 2022 US dollar, purchaser price, by 6-digit NAICS industry. data.gov record. v1.4.0 (Zenodo) was downloaded for comparison only, because it is not an EPA release.

Snapshot

The headline numbers use BCRED’s 10-Q for the period ended June 30, 2026 (SEC accession 0001803498-26-000048). The sector view also covers all 16 filings from September 30, 2022 onward. That is as far back as the SEC BDC Data Sets go.

Each filing’s sector rows add up to BCRED’s reported total investments at fair value. The holdings table is $10.3m (0.013%) short of that total: one holding’s XBRL tag contains stray tab characters, which split its row in the SEC file.

The metric

Exposure-weighted sector emissions-intensity indicator: each mapped industry’s EPA emission factor, weighted by that industry’s share of BCRED’s fair value in mapped industries, summed.

\[ \text{indicator} = \sum_i \frac{FV_i}{\sum_{j \in \text{mapped}} FV_j} \times \text{factor}_i \]

  • FV = fair value as reported by BCRED, in US dollars. Source: BCRED’s own industry subtotals in the schedule of investments.
  • factor = the simple average of EPA v1.3 “with margins” factors for the NAICS codes in the crosswalk, in kg CO2e per 2022 USD. Shown as t CO2e per $m (× 1,000).
  • Coverage: industries without a factor are excluded from the weights, never treated as zero. Latest coverage is 92.2%.
  • Tiers: High / Medium / Low are tertiles of the factor across the 54 mapped industries: Low ≤ 107 t/\(m < Medium ≤ 212 t/\)m < High.

The crosswalk

BCRED’s industry labels were mapped by hand to NAICS-6 codes. The mapping was reviewed and corrected before use. Where an industry maps to several codes, its factor is their simple average. Six labels have no emissions proxy: four are too broad to map without guessing, and EPA v1.3 has no factors for electric power.

Limitations

  1. Proxy, not financed emissions. Sector averages stand in for each borrower’s real emissions. Two companies in the same sector can differ by an order of magnitude.
  2. Sector-level, not issuer-level. BCRED tags industry only on subtotals, not on individual holdings, so no company gets its own factor. The holdings explorer has no industry column for this reason.
  3. Snapshot date. Holdings are as of June 30, 2026, reported in a filing made months later and downloaded on November 5, 2026. The portfolio will have changed since.
  4. No Blackstone private data. Only public SEC filings and public EPA data are used. Nothing here comes from Blackstone systems or non-public information.
  5. Dollars aren’t like-for-like. EPA factors are per 2022 dollar of purchases; BCRED’s values are 2026 dollars of investment. No inflation adjustment is made, and invested capital isn’t spending.
  6. Mapping judgment. Industry-to-NAICS choices are judgment calls, each rated for confidence. Professional Services and Commercial Services are large and rated low.
  7. Unmapped exposure. 7.8% of fair value has no factor, mostly BCRED’s “not assigned” bucket and its broad “Consumer” label.
  8. SEC data quirks. Two SOI columns are mislabelled by the SEC (fair value and cost), and a few rows are malformed. Both were checked against XBRL tags and documented.

Every assumption is logged, with the reason, in docs-notes/DECISIONS.md in the project repository.
